# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The YouTube to MP3 & MP4 community welcomes everyone, regardless of age, disability, background, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation. We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and make room for different perspectives.
- Offer constructive feedback on ideas and code, and accept feedback with an open mind.
- Help newcomers and respect other people's time and contributions.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries, and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual comments, imagery, or advances.
- Sharing another person's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, posting spam, or continuing to contact someone after being asked to stop.
- Retaliating against or intimidating anyone who reports a violation or assists with its investigation.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and official community events and channels. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting a violation

Report violations privately to the project maintainers at **irvanadwifangga@gmail.com**. Include a description of what happened, when it happened, relevant links or evidence, and a safe way to contact you. Do not open a public issue containing identities or sensitive details.

Maintainers will protect the confidentiality of reports and the people involved as far as reasonably possible while handling reports fairly. Information will be shared only with those who need it to handle the report. A maintainer involved in a report must not investigate or decide their own case; where possible, another uninvolved maintainer will handle it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will assess reports based on context, impact, and any history of misconduct. When safe and appropriate, the people involved will have an opportunity to explain what happened. Actions may include:

1. A request to correct behavior or remove inappropriate content.
2. A written warning with clear limits on further interaction.
3. A temporary restriction on participation.
4. A permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Serious violations may result in immediate restrictions or a ban. Maintainers will communicate decisions and reasons to the people involved without disclosing unnecessary personal information. Appeals may be submitted privately to the same reporting address, with additional information for review.
